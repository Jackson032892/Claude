## 3. Custom box with no MOQ on the nail product: what's actually possible

**Product MOQ: yes, you can avoid it.**
- Many Alibaba and all AliExpress listings are "ready stock" with MOQ 1 to 10. Buy 30 to 50 units that way for the test.
- Factories will waive their stated MOQ for a paid trial if you say scale is coming and pay 10 to 20% over the tier price (message 2E). This is normal.
- Dropship route: CJ or your sourcing agent holds the units and ships one per order; they'll also do the packing step below.

**Box MOQ: nobody in China prints one box, but the floor is low.**
- Alibaba box printers (Guangzhou Xinrong, Guangdong Qicai, Yiwu Fanxi and dozens more) run 100-piece minimums at roughly $0.05 to $0.30 per box for a folding carton, more for rigid magnetic gift boxes. 100 boxes is a $30 to $80 spend.
- US on-demand printers: Packola has no minimum, Arka starts at 10 units, Packlane is low-minimum. Expect $1.50 to $4 per box at those quantities, but you get it in a week with a 3D proof.
- Most polish OEM suppliers bundle a custom box at 300 to 500 units. That's the real "phase 2" order.

**Zero-MOQ branded box for the test phase (do this now, not the custom print):**
1. Ask the supplier for their plain white folding box (they all have one).
2. Order 50 to 100 premium die-cut stickers with your logo (Sticker Mule, Vistaprint AU, any qty) and a matching thank-you card. Apply to the white box. It photographs as a branded product and costs under $60.
3. Optional: a custom printed belly band or sleeve from a Brisbane print shop, also no minimum.
4. After the test hits (your rule: 50K+ views and growing, or the first 20 orders), place 300 to 500 custom boxes bundled with the polish reorder.

That sequence follows your own custom-product rule: never build custom until it's proven to convert.

## 4. What you're missing (do these this week)

1. **Order samples today, all three, from AliExpress.** You cannot film without product in hand and Alibaba samples take longer. Two polish shades plus a magnet, one sinus mask, one hammock. 7 to 14 days to Brisbane.
2. **Shipping physics decides two of these.** Nail polish is a flammable liquid (many single-parcel lines refuse it), the sinus mask has a lithium battery (battery line only), the hammock is bulky (volumetric weight can add $8 to $15 per US order). Every quote must state the shipping line, and US warehouse stock is a big plus.
3. **Claims and compliance.** The sinus mask is a health device: write "comfort, warmth, relief" not "cures sinusitis", and expect Meta and TikTok ad review to be stricter than organic. The hammock needs a stated weight rating and hardware in the box, or returns will eat you. Polish needs an MSDS.
4. **Store plan.** The hammock goes straight onto Mistle Paws (same niche, USD, shipping protection and warranty add-ons already built). Nail polish and sinus mask each need their own brand and Shopify store, which also means two logos and two domains.
5. **Brand names, trademark and domain check** before any box or sticker is printed.
6. **Competitor velocity check** (your mentorship trick): place an order on the purrch, sinulia and magnetique stores and read the Shopify order number.
7. **Saturation check on TikTok, not just Instagram**, for all three. The hammock in particular.
8. **Supplier tracker**: 5 to 10 suppliers per product, quote, MOQ, lead time, sample ordered, shipping line, warehouse. Kill anyone above average.

- **Nail polish (your winner).** Run the gifting angle magnetique is already proving, but make the magnet pass the transformation inside the video: plain polish, magnet hovers, the cat-eye line snaps into place. That's your "wow, these people can market" moment with no cuts. Second format: "the cat-eye a man designed vs." (it's their best reel, so copy the emotion, not the shots). Third: "Sephora nail girlies" comparison, cost per manicure vs salon cat-eye. Target 25+ women, no POV, no link-in-bio in the caption.
- **Sinus mask.** Pattern interrupt is the red glow on a face. Open on the mask already lit in a dark room, then the relief. Sufferer POV, morning congestion, allergy season, "every sinusitis sufferer needs this". Comment-reply videos will feed this one for months because people describe their symptoms in the comments.
- **Cat hammock.** The prototype format is burning fast because of purrch's 448K reel. Either move this week with your own concept-vs-reality version on Mistle Paws, or differentiate with a variant (window-mount, radiator-mount, a second colour). Cat-mom targeting, soft pacing, slow movement.
