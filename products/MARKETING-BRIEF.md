# Marketing brief: Sinuglow, Lumia Nails, Cloudpurr (24 Sep 2026)

## What the research says

**Competitor formats that print (pulled from their top reels):**
- Sinulia (1,093 followers) has two reels over 1M views (1.18M and 1.03M) with the exact same video: a guy with red, puffy, watery eyes holds the band up to camera, text "Every SINUSITIS Sufferer Needs This", puts it on, the LEDs glow. Same guy, same text, different room. The format is doing the work, not the account. That's a green light for a fresh page.
- Purrch's 4.1M-view reel (1.0M likes) is the prototype gag: "The idea 🤮" with a cat and a bucket in a beautiful living room, then the real hammock. It's a joke first, product second.
- Magnetique's best is "The cat eye a man designed ❌ VS." (pencil in a polish bottle), then the real cat-eye. The gift angle ("Girls are so hard to gift") is their second format.

**Real customer language, sinus devices (Trustpilot, 270 reviews across NoMore and Soovera):**
- Praise: "I can breathe out of both nostrils", "I can smell much better", "literally drains my sinus", "relief after just 20 minutes", "same heat as long as I use it" (heat bags cool down), "finally slept", "avoided surgery", "tried every remedy for 11 years".
- Complaints: "hardly any heat", "won't turn on", "battery died after 12 months", "eyes got too hot" (full eye masks), "day 3 and no change", refunds and customer service from China.
- Who buys: chronic sufferers who have tried neti pots, Flonase, steam, Sudafed, antibiotics, and have an ENT surgery quote. Desperate and skeptical at the same time.

**Timing:** ragweed season is on now (Sept to Oct), cold and flu season starts October, "sinus pressure" searches peak Nov to Feb. Sinuglow is a Q4 product. Lumia and Cloudpurr are gifts, so Christmas content from 1 Nov.

**Market size:** chronic sinusitis hits roughly 1 in 8 adults, allergic rhinitis tens of millions more in the US alone.

## SINUGLOW (deepest)

**Core insight:** the buyer doesn't want another spray or rinse. They want something drug-free that they can FEEL working in the first minute (heat is instant sensory proof) and that doesn't tie them to a cord or a microwave.

**Positioning:** Red light and heat right on the sinuses. 15 minutes a day, drug-free, cordless. Keep Sinulia's "15 min a day, drug-free" line, it's working.

**Claims rule:** relief, comfort, warmth, drainage, "breathe easier". Never "cures sinusitis" or "treats infection". Keeps Meta and TikTok off your back.

**Offer:** $49.99 single, $79.99 two-pack ("buy two, trust me"), 90-day guarantee, free shipping.

### Angles
1. Sufferer POV (proven): the sick face, the reveal, the glow. Our version: a woman 35+, dark bedroom, tissue box.
2. Nasal spray rebound: "the reason your nasal spray stopped working". Afrin addiction is a huge topic and it's controversy that puts the advertiser, not the product, in the hot seat.
3. Snoring partner: "my husband snores because he can't breathe through his nose". Women buying for men, highest purchase intent on the platform.
4. Allergy season right now: "ragweed season hit and I'm not doing another month of this".
5. "Tried everything" list: neti pot, steam, Flonase, antibiotics, ENT quote. Fits the trending deadpan "reasons to…" list format this month.
6. Smell loss: "I haven't smelled my coffee in 3 years".
7. Sinus headache / face pain: "if your face hurts when you bend over".
8. Travel: "the only thing that got me through a flight with a sinus infection".

### Hooks (text on screen)
1. Every sinusitis sufferer needs this (keep in rotation, it's proven)
2. POV: you can finally breathe out of BOTH nostrils
3. The reason your nasal spray stopped working
4. I haven't smelled my coffee in 3 years
5. My husband snores because he can't breathe through his nose. Not anymore.
6. Neti pot girlies, put it down
7. Things I tried before this: (list) … then 15 minutes with this
8. If your face hurts when you bend over, watch this
9. Ragweed season is here and I'm not doing another month of this
10. Waking up with a sinus headache every day is not normal
11. A warm compress that actually stays warm
12. The $30 thing I use instead of my $2,000 ENT consult

### Concepts (first frame, the turn, caption, score for a fresh page)
1. **Sick face, our version** (proven format). First frame: woman, puffy red eyes, tissue box, dark bedroom, band held toward camera. Turn: band goes on, LEDs glow red in the dark room, her face relaxes, one deep nose breath. Caption: "Comment RELIEF and I'll send it". 9/10.
2. **Glow in the dark** (cinematic). First frame: pitch-black bedroom, just two rows of red dots floating. Turn: camera pulls back, it's someone asleep breathing through their nose, partner beside them with no earplugs. Caption: "the only night light I need". 8/10.
3. **Reasons I've given up on my sinuses** (trend adaptation). First frame: on the couch counting on fingers: neti pot, steam, Flonase, three rounds of antibiotics, ENT quote. Turn: "and then a $30 headband did this", band on, glow. Caption: "not medical advice, just my face". 8/10.
4. **Stolen** (couples, sells the 2-pack). First frame: she puts the band on. Turn: he walks in, takes it off her face, puts it on his. Cut to two bands charging side by side. Caption: "buy two, trust me". 7.5/10.
5. **Bend-over test** (works muted). First frame: bends to tie shoes, winces, grabs face. Turn: 15 minutes later, bends again, no wince, big nose breath. Caption: "if you know, you know". 8/10.
6. **Comment reply engine.** Reply to real comments ("does it work for a deviated septum?", "how long per day?") with 10-second answers. This one feeds the page for months because sufferers describe their symptoms in the comments.

## LUMIA NAILS

**Insight:** cat-eye is the number one nail design of 2026 (silver cat-eye alone has 12.7M TikTok plays). The magnet moment is the product demo and the hook in one, and it works muted. Magnetique's twist is the gift angle: a man buying it.

**Positioning:** salon cat-eye at home, the pen does the magnet and the light. Sephora-level feel, $19.99, gift box.

**Angles:** gift from him (Q4 gold), salon price vs pen, "man designed vs", satisfying magnet reveal, "no salon, no lamp".

**Hooks:** Tag your gf if she needs this (proven). The cat eye a man designed vs the one a woman designed. POV: your nails cost $12 and look like $80. I stopped paying $85 for cat-eye. Watch the magnet do its thing. Sephora nail girlies, this was made for you. Christmas gift for her: solved.

**Concepts:** (1) macro magnet reveal, extreme close-up of wet polish, magnet hovers, line snaps into place, no cuts. (2) Gift unboxing from the guy's POV, her reaction. (3) Salon receipt vs the pen. (4) "Reasons to get cat-eye nails" list trend.

## CLOUDPURR

**Insight:** Purrch went to 4.1M views with a joke, not a product shot. Ugly prototype in a gorgeous room, then the real thing. Copy the emotion (absurd to beautiful), not the shots.

**Positioning:** cat furniture that doesn't look like cat furniture. Cat mom aesthetic, $39.99 on Mistle Paws.

**Angles:** prototype gag, "my cat sleeps on my clean laundry" pain, matches-my-living-room decor, cat tax, gift for the cat mom.

**Hooks:** Don't show this to a cat mom (proven). The idea 🤮 then the product 😍. My cat has claimed every chair in the house. Cat furniture that doesn't look like cat furniture. POV: your cat finally stops sleeping on your laundry. Things my cat ignores vs this.

**Concepts:** (1) prototype trilogy: bucket, basket, hammock. (2) Cat discovers it, slow pacing, soft audio. (3) Home tour where the hammock is the reveal. (4) Comment reply: "will it hold a 20 lb cat?" then a bag-of-flour weight test.

## Test plan
- Sinuglow first: season plus one proven format. Then Lumia, then Cloudpurr on Mistle Paws.
- 2 posts a day per platform, 2 weeks minimum per product, rotate 3 to 4 angles, test one new format a day.
- Benchmark: 1 comment per 1,000 views. Below that after 10 posts, change angle. Above it, spam variations of that video.
- Comment CTA words: RELIEF (sinus), NAILS (Lumia), CAT (Cloudpurr). No "link in bio" in captions.

## SINUGLOW: audiences beyond sinusitis (added 24 Sep)

Sell the symptom (blocked nose, face pressure, can't sleep), not the diagnosis. Hay fever (allergic rhinitis) inflames the nasal lining, blocks sinus drainage and is a major risk factor for sinusitis, so it's the same buyer with a bigger pool: 10 to 20% of people vs about 31M sinusitis cases a year in the US.

| Audience | Why it fits | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Hay fever / allergies | Biggest pool; US ragweed now, AU spring now | Every hay fever sufferer needs this |
| Cold and flu season | Oct to Feb, universal | When the cold is gone but your nose didn't get the memo |
| Snorers and their partners | Blocked nose forces mouth breathing and snoring | My husband snores because he can't breathe through his nose |
| Pet allergy owners | Won't rehome the pet; shares Cloudpurr's cat-mom audience | Allergic to my cat. Not getting rid of her. |
| Winter heating / dry air | Heating dries and thickens mucus | Why your nose blocks up every time the heater's on |
| One blocked nostril / deviated septum | Huge, relatable, funny | Only one nostril works at night. Why is it always the one I'm lying on? |
| Smell loss after a cold | Emotional | I haven't smelled my coffee in 3 years |
| Frequent flyers | Pressure on descent | The only thing that got me through a flight with a sinus infection |

Avoid:
- Pregnancy congestion (20 to 30% of pregnant women get it). Heat and light device, unknown safety, not worth the liability.
- Leading with "sinus headache". 75 to 95% of self-diagnosed sinus headaches are actually migraines, so it invites "didn't work" refunds. Say "face pressure" instead.
- Nasal polyps and deviated septum as medical claims. Use them only as relatable symptoms.
